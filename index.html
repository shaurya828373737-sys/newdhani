Why no predictor can beat a fair 50/50 game

The math, in plain language

🎲

Independence

Each round in a fair RNG game is independent — the coin has no memory. P(BIG | last 5 BIG) is still exactly P(BIG). No "trend" or "pattern" in the past changes the next outcome.

📉

Law of large numbers

Run any 50/50 algorithm long enough and its hit rate converges to 50%. That's not an opinion; it's a theorem. The chart above is a live demo.

🧠

Gambler's fallacy

The belief that "after 5 BIGs, SMALL is overdue" feels intuitive but is mathematically false on independent events. Algorithm 03 is built on this fallacy on purpose — watch it fail.

⚠️

Real "color prediction" apps

Many platforms (WinGo, 91 Club, Tiranga, dhaniwin and similar) are not even fair RNGs — the operator can bias outcomes. There, no predictor's long-run accuracy can be even 50%.

💸

Why "98% accurate" tools exist

They sell ads, referrals, or "premium" plans. The product is not a prediction — the product is the user. If a real 98% tool existed, it would be quietly making its owner rich, not sold to strangers.
